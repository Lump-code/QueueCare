    About the project

    In many Indian government hospitals, patients queue from early morning to get an OPD slip — then queue again for the doctor, the lab and the pharmacy. A single visit can take 4–5 hours.

    The Healthcare Access Project digitises this journey: online registration with ID verification, timed OPD tokens, live queue tracking, lab and pharmacy tokens, and an operations dashboard for administrators.

    It is designed to work alongside ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) and on low-end phones and help-desk kiosks.

    Solutions

    📝 Online registration

    Register from home with Aadhaar/ABHA OTP verification.

    🎫 Timed QR tokens

    Each patient gets a slot and QR token — no early-morning lines.

    👥 Live queues

    Counter displays, call-next and alerts keep patients informed.

    🧪 Diagnostics

    Pre-booked lab slots and online report status.

    💊 Pharmacy tokens

    Medicines packed ahead; collect when notified.

    📊 Load analytics

    Occupancy, beds and peak hours in real time.
